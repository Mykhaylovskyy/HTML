It's Oliver

Oliver is a lively ginger cat with a soft, striped coat that shines like the sunset. He wakes up early, stretches proudly, and meows for breakfast. Curious by nature, he loves chasing toy mice and watching birds from the window. In the evenings, Oliver curls up on the couch, purring like a little engine. His green eyes sparkle with mischief, but he’s also gentle and affectionate, making everyone in the house smile.
